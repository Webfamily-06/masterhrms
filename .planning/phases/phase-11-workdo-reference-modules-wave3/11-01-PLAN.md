# Phase 11 Plan: Internal WorkDo Add-on Modules Verification (Wave 3: Knowledge, Learning & Operations)

## Goal
Empirically verify 10 internal WorkDo reference add-on modules currently categorized as `PLANNED` across Newsletter, Feedback, Knowledge Base, Diagram, Portfolio, Resume Builder, Spreadsheet, Innovation Center, Business Model Canvas, and Marketing Plan by authoring and executing comprehensive native automated test suites (`node:test` + `node:assert/strict`), promoting all 10 items to `VERIFIED` (Milestone 7.6).

## Target Scope & Requirements

1. **REQ-W3-01 (`ADDON-048` - Newsletter)**:
   - Subscriber management (email, name, subscription status `SUBSCRIBED`/`UNSUBSCRIBED`, tags, source).
   - Newsletter campaign broadcasts (subject, preview text, HTML content, status `DRAFT`/`SCHEDULED`/`SENT`, metrics).
   - Unsubscribe lifecycle and multi-tenant isolation.

2. **REQ-W3-02 (`ADDON-049` - Feedback)**:
   - Multi-channel feedback submissions (customer/employee/product, rating 1-5 stars, category, sentiment analysis).
   - Review and resolution workflow (status `NEW`, `IN_REVIEW`, `RESOLVED`, `ARCHIVED` with admin resolution notes).
   - Aggregated feedback analytics (average rating, distribution, sentiment breakdown).
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

3. **REQ-W3-03 (`ADDON-054` - Knowledge Base)**:
   - Category taxonomy and article authoring (slug, title, rich content, tags, status `DRAFT`/`PUBLISHED`).
   - Keyword and category search, view count tracking, and helpfulness voting (thumbs up/down).
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

4. **REQ-W3-04 (`ADDON-055` - Diagram)**:
   - Architecture diagram and flowchart storage (nodes, edges, node coordinates, labels, diagramType `FLOWCHART`/`ERD`/`UML`/`ARCHITECTURE`).
   - Canvas configuration, zoom/grid layout metadata, and versioning.
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

5. **REQ-W3-05 (`ADDON-056` - Portfolio)**:
   - Showcase project creation (title, client name, category, completion date, media attachments, technology tags).
   - Client testimonial endorsement and visibility flags (`DRAFT`, `FEATURED`, `PUBLISHED`).
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

6. **REQ-W3-06 (`ADDON-057` - Resume Builder)**:
   - Candidate/Employee CV builder (profile summary, work history, education, skills matrix, certifications).
   - ATS-friendly structured export schema and design template selection (`MODERN`, `CLASSIC`, `TECH_EXECUTIVE`).
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

7. **REQ-W3-07 (`ADDON-061` - Spreadsheet)**:
   - Multi-sheet workbook documents (sheets, rows, columns, cell grid data).
   - Cell formulas (e.g. `=SUM(A1:A10)`), formatting attributes (bold, color, background), and cell revision versioning.
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

8. **REQ-W3-08 (`ADDON-062` - Innovation Center)**:
   - Canonical route wiring for `/api/innovation` and `/api/v1/innovation` to `suggestionBoxRouter`.
   - Idea proposal submission, community upvotes, view count tracking, and status progression (`New` -> `Under Review` -> `Accepted` -> `Complete`).
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

9. **REQ-W3-09 (`ADDON-064` - Business Model Canvas)**:
   - Osterwalder's 9-building-blocks canvas template in `StrategyStudioService` (`key_partners`, `key_activities`, `key_resources`, `value_propositions`, `customer_relationships`, `channels`, `customer_segments`, `cost_structure`, `revenue_streams`).
   - Document creation, quadrant item updates, impact scores, and planning strategy integration.
   - Multi-tenant isolation.

10. **REQ-W3-10 (`ADDON-065` - Marketing Plan)**:
    - Canonical route wiring for `/api/marketing-plan` and `/api/v1/marketing-plan`.
    - Marketing campaign planning (`MarketingCampaign` in Prisma) with budgets, spend tracking, channels, target audience, and dates.
    - Marketing plan strategy integration in `PlanningStrategyService` (type `marketing_plan`).
    - Multi-tenant isolation.
