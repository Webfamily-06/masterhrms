## Target Scope & Requirements

1. **REQ-W5-01 (`ADDON-051` - Restaurant Menu)**:
   - Provide Restaurant Menu catalog structure in `RestaurantMenuService`: menu categories (Starters, Mains, Desserts, Beverages), items with pricing, dietary badges (Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal, Chef Special), availability toggle, and digital QR menu payload.
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.

2. **REQ-W5-02 (`ADDON-052` - Google Leads)**:
   - Provide Google Ads / Lead Form webhook ingestion in `GoogleLeadsService`: webhook payload parser, UTM campaign attribution, automatic conversion into `CrmContact` or Lead record with duplicate check.
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.

3. **REQ-W5-03 (`ADDON-099` - Job Search / Job Board)**:
   - Provide public Job Board listings in `JobBoardService`: published job posts query, applicant job application submission with resume and screening question responses, and status progression (`applied` -> `shortlisted` -> `rejected`).
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.

4. **REQ-W5-04 (`ADDON-100` - LMS / Learning Management System)**:
   - Provide Course & Learning Management in `LmsService`: course catalog, modules & lessons, employee course enrollment, lesson progress tracking, quiz score recording, and course completion certificate issuance.
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.

5. **REQ-W5-05 (`ADDON-101` - eBook Library)**:
   - Provide Corporate Digital Library in `EbookService`: eBook title catalog, category taxonomy, digital file metadata (PDF/EPUB), employee bookmarks, reading progress tracking, and access permission checks.
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.

6. **REQ-W5-06 (`ADDON-106` - Roadmap Central)**:
   - Provide Product / Organizational Roadmap in `RoadmapCentralService`: quarterly horizons (Q1-Q4), strategic epics/features, delivery status (`planned`, `in_progress`, `delivered`), priority, and stakeholder feedback upvoting.
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.

7. **REQ-W5-07 (`ADDON-109` - Request Management)**:
   - Provide Internal Employee General Service Request lifecycle in `RequestManagementService`: category (Stationery, Hardware, Travel, General), request submission, approval routing, status workflow (`pending`, `under_review`, `approved`, `fulfilled`), and SLA resolution tracking.
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.

8. **REQ-W5-08 (`ADDON-111` - Sidebar Menu Builder)**:
   - Provide Navigation Customizer in `SidebarMenuService`: custom menu navigation layout, group ordering, role-based visibility (Admin, Manager, Employee), active status toggles, and icon/route configurations.
   - Strict multi-tenant isolation.
