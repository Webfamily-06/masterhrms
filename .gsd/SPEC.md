# MasterHRMS Enterprise Specification

## Vision
Comprehensive enterprise Human Resource Management System (HRMS) and multi-tenant ERP platform providing 100% feature parity with WorkDo Dash SaaS, including full HRM, Recruitment, Payroll, Attendance, CRM, Project Management, and all 133 modular add-ons and external gateways.

## Scope & Architectural Principles
- Full multi-tenant isolation with subdomains and tenant scoping.
- Modular pluggable add-on architecture with 133 canonical reference modules.
- End-to-end statutory payroll calculations, biometric attendance ingestion, and double-entry accounting.
- Comprehensive external integrations (Google Workspace, WhatsApp, Twilio, Slack, Jira, QuickBooks, Xero, Plaid, ZATCA e-Invoicing).
