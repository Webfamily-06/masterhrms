# MASTERHRMS — Phase P7 Business Rule Decisions

## Authority Reference
All decisions below are derived from authoritative MASTERHRMS specifications (`00_Architecture_and_Conventions.md`, `01_HR_Panel_Spec.md`, `02_Employee_Portal_Spec.md`, and `03_Interlinking_and_Realtime_Flows.md`).

---

### Decision Register

| Decision ID | Domain | Business Rule | Decision & Locking Formulation | Affected Models | Open/Locked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P7-BR-001** | Training | Training Programs & Types | Standard default categories: Compliance, Technical, Leadership, Soft Skills. Programs require title, duration in hours, passing score (default 80%), and mandatory flag. | `TrainingCourse`, `TrainingTypeMaster` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-002** | Training | Session Capacity & Conflicts | Training sessions must specify trainer, date range, location/room, and positive capacity. Duplicate employee registration in the same session is strictly blocked. If session is full, additional registrations are rejected. | `TrainingSession`, `SessionAttendance` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-003** | Training | Completion & Certificates | An enrollment is marked `completed` when progress reaches 100% and score >= passingScore. Generates a unique certificate ID (`CERT-XXXXX`) with certified date. | `CourseEnrollment` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-004** | Training | Mandatory vs Optional | Mandatory trainings flag a non-compliance alert for employees until completed. HR dashboard aggregates mandatory completion percentage across departments. | `TrainingCourse`, `CourseEnrollment` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-005** | Assets | Asset Lifecycle States | Authoritative asset states: `available` → `assigned` → `maintenance` → `retired` → `disposed` → `lost`. Only `available` assets can be assigned. | `Asset`, `AssetAssignment` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-006** | Assets | Assignment & Digital Sign-off | Asset assignment creates an `AssetAssignment` record and transitions Asset status to `assigned`. Employee must acknowledge receipt via digital sign-off in ESS (`acknowledgedAt`). Returning an asset records condition on return and returns Asset to `available`. | `Asset`, `AssetAssignment` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-007** | Assets | Depreciation Engine | Default method: **Straight-Line Method (SLM)**. Formula: `Monthly Depreciation = (purchasePrice - salvageValue) / usefulLifeMonths`. Current book value is calculated as `purchasePrice - (monthsSincePurchase * monthlyDepreciation)`. Depreciated value cannot drop below salvageValue. | `Asset`, `AssetDepreciationSchedule` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-008** | Assets | Maintenance & Repair | Logging maintenance records service type (preventive, corrective), service vendor, cost, and next scheduled service date. Asset status shifts to `maintenance` during active servicing and reverts to `available` upon completion. | `Asset`, `AssetMaintenance` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-009** | Assets | Employee Asset Requests | Employees may submit asset requests with category, item name, urgency priority, and business justification. HR/Admin reviews with `approved`, `rejected`, or `fulfilled` statuses. | `AssetRequest` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-010** | Meetings | Conflict Detection Engine | The server enforces conflict validation before creating a meeting. It rejects meetings if: (a) the selected room has an overlapping meeting in `[startTime, endTime]`, or (b) the organizer has another overlapping meeting. | `Meeting`, `MeetingRoom` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-011** | Meetings | Room Amenities & Capacity | Conference rooms track name, location, seating capacity, amenities (e.g. Video Conferencing, Whiteboard, TV), and active status. Over-capacity bookings produce a warning. | `MeetingRoom` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-012** | Meetings | RSVP State Management | Meeting attendees receive an invitation with default RSVP status `pending`. Attendees can respond with `accepted`, `declined`, or `tentative`. Responses are recorded with timestamp. | `MeetingAttendee` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-013** | Meetings | Meeting Action Items | Meetings can generate actionable tasks with title, description, assigned employee, due date, priority (low, medium, high, urgent), and status (`open`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `cancelled`). Assignee can update status directly from ESS. | `MeetingActionItem` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-014** | Documents | Document Vault & Access Scopes | Documents are categorized into Employment Contract, NDA & IP Agreement, Company Policy, Statutory & Tax, and Academic Certificate. Policies are visible company-wide; employee contracts and identity documents are scoped to SELF, HR, and Admin. | `CompanyDocument`, `DocumentCategory` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-015** | Documents | Employee Contracts & E-Sign | Contracts link to `ContractType` and contain start date, end date, compensation, and legal terms. Digital signature records signer name, signature data URL, IP address, and timestamp (`signedAt`). | `EmployeeContract`, `ContractType` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-016** | Documents | Policy Sign-Off Campaigns | Mandatory company policies track employee acknowledgements. Employees must review and submit digital confirmation. HR monitors compliance percent across departments. | `DocumentAcknowledgement` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-017** | Documents | Token Merge Letters Engine | The Letters Center dynamically generates official HR letters using safe token replacement. Allowed tokens: `{{employeeName}}`, `{{employeeCode}}`, `{{designation}}`, `{{department}}`, `{{joiningDate}}`, `{{currentDate}}`, `{{companyName}}`. | `GeneratedLetter`, `DocumentTemplate` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-018** | Documents | Employee Letter Requests | Employees can request official letters (Bonafide, Experience, NOC, Salary Certificate) with purpose notes. HR fulfills by generating the letter or rejecting with explanation. | `DocumentRequest`, `GeneratedLetter` | **LOCKED** |
| **P7-BR-019** | Documents | Media & File Immutability | All document uploads and generated letter records are immutable. Revisions require creating a new version record. Files are tenant-isolated and encrypted. | `StoredDocument`, `CompanyDocument` | **LOCKED** |
