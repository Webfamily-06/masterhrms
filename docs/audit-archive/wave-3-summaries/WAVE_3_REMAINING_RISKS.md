## 2. Risk Summary & Recommendations

None of the identified gaps represent critical data corruption risks or block the formal closure of Wave 3:
1. **Financial Integrity:** Double-entry journal equality, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet, and stock decrements are strictly enforced with zero mathematical discrepancies.
2. **Security & Isolation:** Single-schema multi-tenant isolation is verified across all endpoints with fail-closed security.
3. **Actionable Handover to Wave 4:** The items marked **Medium** (TypeScript compilation errors in `projects.routes.ts`, `_prisma_migrations` tracking, and `tenant-models.config.ts` registration) are designated for immediate resolution in **Step 4.0** before starting CRM and Project feature work.
