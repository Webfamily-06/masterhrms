Step 3: Once you complete this process, go to the company side, select any of the modules, and click on the Create button. For example, if you want to create an item, click on the “+” button given at the top. On that page, you will see a button called “generate with AI” at the top.

Step 4: Click on the button, and select the language, tone, level of AI creativity, number of results you want, maximum result length, product name, audience, and a short description regarding your product.

Step 5: Once you add all of these details, click on the generate button, and your content will be generated easily. You can also copy the text, or copy the selected text as per your convenience.
