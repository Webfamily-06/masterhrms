Introduction
The HRM Add-On helps you manage your entire team in one place, from the day someone joins to the day they leave. It covers everyday HR tasks like keeping employee records, tracking attendance, managing leave, running payroll, and organizing shifts. It also lets you recognize good work through awards and promotions, and handle issues like complaints and warnings when they come up. You can customize things like branches, departments, and job titles to match how your company is set up. Simple dashboards and calendars make it easy to see what’s happening and manage your team without hassle.

How to Use The HRM Add-On?
HRM Dashboard
The HRM Dashboard gives a company-wide overview of your attendance and HR activity through a set of summary cards, panels, and a calendar. Each summary card is clickable and takes you straight to its related page for a closer look.
Eight cards across the top summarize key metrics. Total Employees shows the number of active employees and opens the Employees page, and Present Today shows how many are present, along with the day’s attendance rate, and opens the Attendance page.
Absent Today shows how many are absent with the change from the previous day and opens the Attendance page, and On Leave shows how many are on leave along with the number of pending approvals and opens the Leave page. The second row covers Total Branch and Total Department across all branches, Total Promotions for the current month, and Terminations for the current month, each opening its respective page when clicked.
The Department Distribution panel lists each department with its branch and shows the number of employees in it, with a bar indicating the relative size of each department.
The Quick Actions panel provides shortcuts to common tasks: Add New Employee, Mark Attendance, Apply for Leave, Process Payroll, Create Promotion, and Create Resignation, each taking you straight to the relevant page.
The Employees on Leave panel shows the list of employees on leave today or displays a “No employees on leave today” message if there are none. The Missing Attendance Today panel highlights employees who have not yet marked attendance or confirms when all employees have done so.
The Events & Holidays Calendar displays upcoming events and holidays as colored bars on a calendar. You can move between periods using the arrows and the “Today” button or switch between Month, Week, and Day views.
The Recent Leave Applications panel lists the latest leave requests, each showing the employee name, leave type, date range, and status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected). The Announcements panel lists recent announcements, each showing the title, a short description, and the date.

Manage Employees
To add a new employee, click the “Create” button on the Employee page. The form is divided into three tabs, Basic Info, Contact & Banking, and Hours & Docs; that you fill in one after another using the “Next” button.
The Basic Info tab covers Personal Information and Employment Information. The Employee ID is auto-generated, so select the Date of Birth and choose the Gender as Male, Female, or Other using the radio buttons.
Then select the User (only company users are applicable here, and you can add one using the “Create user” link), choose the Shift (or add one with the “Create shift” link), set a Date of Joining, and pick the Employment Type.
Once a user is selected, the Branch dropdown becomes available. Choose the branch from it (branches are populated from system setup), or click the “Create branch” link below the field if the branch you need doesn’t exist yet.
Once a branch is selected, choose the Department from the Department dropdown, which becomes available only after a branch is picked (departments are populated from system setup).
Once a department is selected, choose the Designation from the Designation dropdown, which becomes available only after a department is picked (designations are populated from system setup).
The Contact & Banking tab covers Contact Information, Emergency Contact, and Banking Information. Fill in Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, State, Country, and Postal Code, along with the Emergency Contact Name, Relationship, and Phone Number.
Then enter the Bank Name, Account Holder Name, Account Number, Bank Identifier Code, Bank Branch, and the optional Tax Payer Id.
The Hours & Docs tab covers Hours & Rates and Employee Documents. Enter the Basic Salary, Hours Per Day, Days Per Week, and Rate Per Hour. Under Employee Documents.
Select the Document Type and upload the Document File using “Choose File” (add a type first with the “Create document type” link if needed). Click “Add Document” to attach more items, then click “Create” to save the employee.
In Grid view, each card displays the employee’s Employment Type badge (Contract, Full Time, Part Time, Temporary), Avatar, Name, Employee Id (as a link), Branch, Department, Designation, DOB, Date Of Joining, and Action icons for View, Edit, and Delete.
When a user account is disabled, the Actions are replaced by a lock icon with a “User is disabled” note, and these options stay unavailable until the account is enabled again.
Use the Search option to locate specific employees, with options for List View or Grid View at the top, or open the Filters panel to refine results by Branch, Department, Employment Type, and Gender. Click “Apply” to apply the selected filters or “Clear” to reset them.
When you click the View button, it opens the full employee profile across five tabs: Employee Details (employment and contact information), Banking Information, Payslip (displays payslips for all months since the employee’s joining month), Attendance (a log of Date, Clock In, Clock Out, and Overtime Hours), and Documents.
A Quick Actions panel on the right links to the employee’s history, Awards, Warnings, Transfers, Promotions, and Complaints. Clicking any of these takes you to that section’s page, showing only the records for that particular employee.
A “This Month” panel summarizes attendance stats such as Working Days, Present, Absent, Late Arrivals, Early Exits, Overtime, and Unpaid Leave.

Attendance
Attendances
To add a new Attendance entry, click the “Create” button on the Manage Attendances page. Select the Employee (use the “Create employee here” link if the employee doesn’t exist yet) and the Date, then set the Clock In Time and Clock Out Time. You can add any extra details in the Notes.
The page displays a monthly Attendance Report in a grid format. The first column lists each employee with their avatar, name, and designation, followed by a column for every day of the selected month and a Total column that summarizes each employee’s hours for the month.
Each day cell shows a marker indicating the employee’s attendance status for the day, which is automatically determined based on the Clock In and Clock Out times entered. A legend at the top explains every marker: Present, Absent, Half Day, On Leave, Holiday, Day Off, Future, Pending, Late, Early, and Overtime. Hovering over a Day Off marker shows a tooltip labelling it as a non-working day.
Clicking on a day cell opens a popup with that day’s details: Date, Status, entry type (e.g., Late entry), Clock In, Clock Out, and Total Hours, along with a “Click to edit” option to update the entry.
Use the Search option to locate a specific employee and the Month and Year dropdowns at the top right to choose the period you want to view. You can also use the previous and next arrows to move between months.

Shifts
To add a new Shift, click the “Create” button on the Manage Shifts page. Enter the Shift Name, then set the Start Time and End Time along with the Break Start Time and Break End Time. Check “Is Night Shift” if the shift spans overnight.
The page displays all shifts as cards, each showing the Shift Name, the number of Employees assigned, and Edit and Delete icons.
Each card shows three time boxes: Working Time, Break Time, and Total Time, along with the corresponding start and end times. A progress bar below visualizes the full shift span, with the break portion highlighted in yellow within the working time shown in green.
Below the progress bar, the avatars of employees assigned to that shift are listed.

Leave Management
Leave Applications
To create a new Leave Application, click the “Create” button on either the Leave Calendar or the Manage Leave Applications page. Select the Employee (use the “Create employee here” link if the employee doesn’t exist yet).
Select the Leave Type (use the “Create leave type here” link if it doesn’t exist yet). Check “Half Day Leave” if applicable; this reveals a Half Day Type dropdown to choose “Before” or “After”. If left unchecked, an End Date field is displayed instead, allowing you to select a date range. Then enter a Reason, and optionally attach a supporting file.
The Leave Calendar view opens by default, displaying a Monthly Leave Overview in a calendar grid, with each day cell showing the employees on leave that day as colored bars (color-coded by status per the Status Legend: Pending, Approved, Rejected).
If more than two leaves fall on the same day, a “+ more” link shows the rest. Use the arrows or the “Today” button to navigate and toggle between Month, Week, and Day views.
Use the Filters panel on the Leave Calendar to narrow results by Employee, Status, and Leave Type, then click “Apply” to apply the filters or “Clear” to reset them.
On the right, the Pending Approvals panel lists leave requests awaiting action, each showing the Employee name, Leave Type and duration, and date range, with icons to Manage Status ( to open a popup where you can Approve or Reject the request with an optional Comment) or View (see the Leave Application Details) the request. An Upcoming Leaves panel below it lists approved leaves that haven’t started yet.
The Manage Leave Applications page lists all applications in a table with columns for Employee, Leave Type, Start Date, End Date, Days, Applied On, Is Paid, Status, and Actions. Use the Search option to locate a specific leave type. Alternatively, click the Calendar View button to display leave records in a calendar view.
Use the Filters panel to refine results by Employee, Status, Date Range, and Is Paid, then click “Apply” to apply the filters or “Clear” to reset them.
Available actions depend on the application’s status. Pending requests display four icons: Manage Status, which opens a popup to approve or reject the request with an optional Comment; View, to see the full application details; Edit, to update the request; and Delete, to remove the application. For Approved and other finalized requests, only View and Delete are available.

Leave Balance
The Leave Balance page gives a read-only overview of each employee’s leave entitlements. Every employee appears as a separate card showing their avatar and name.
Within each card, a table shows the available leave types, with their Total, Used, and Available values.
The Total column shows the leave allocated for the year, the Used column shows how much has already been taken, and the Available column shows the remaining balance for that leave type.

Leave Types

The list page displays all leave types with columns for Name, Max Days Per Year, Is Paid, and Actions.
Use the Search option to locate a specific leave type and the Filters panel to refine results by payment status under “Is Paid”. Click “Apply” to apply the selected filter or “Clear” to reset it.
Available Actions you can use: View to see the complete leave type details, Edit to update any information, and Delete to remove a leave type that is no longer needed.

Payrolls
Manage Payslip
To create a new payroll, click the “Create” button at the top of the Manage Payrolls page. Enter the title and select the payroll frequency from the dropdown (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly). Then set the pay period start date, pay period end date, and pay date, and choose the bank account from which salaries will be paid. You can add any extra details in the Notes.
The list page displays all payrolls in a table with the Title, Payroll Frequency, Pay Period Start, Pay Period End, Pay Date, Total Net Pay, Employee Count, Status, Payment Status, and Actions columns.
The Status column shows the payroll stage, such as Draft, and the Payment Status column shows whether it is Paid or Unpaid.
Use the search bar at the top to find a payroll by title and the Filters section to narrow the list by Payroll Frequency or Status; select your filter values and click “Apply”, or click “Clear” to reset. You can also switch between list and grid views.
From the Actions column, you can run the payroll (draft status converted into the complete) view to see the details, edit to modify the details, or delete to remove them.
Opening a payroll’s details takes you to the Payroll Details page. The top section summarizes the payroll, its title, pay period, pay date, frequency, and overall status, along with quick-view cards for the total number of Employees, Gross Pay, Deductions, and Net Pay.
hrm
Below that, the Employee Salary Details section lists every employee included in the payroll. Each row shows the employee’s name and email along with their Basic Salary, Allowances, Manual OT, Attendance OT, Deductions, Loans, Gross Pay, Net Pay, and Status. From the Actions column on each row, you can pay the employee’s salary, download the payslip, view the details, or delete the record.
