w14:paraId="7D0C67C9" w14:textId="77777777" w:rsidR="00365680" w:rsidRDefault="00365680" w:rsidP="00365680">The AI Business Advisor Add-On gives every business a daily, AI-written checkup of how their company is performing. It automatically reviews real data from Accounting, HRM, CRM, Projects, POS, and Inventory, scores the overall health of the business out of 100, and uses your connected AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) to generate plain-language Insights, Recommendations, and Alerts.
